Consolidated Student Visualization Vocabulary

DS 2023 | Communicating with Data

Overview¶

  • This vocabulary was co-created by the students of UVA DS 2023 in Fall 2026. 💪

  • Terms were proposed by 13 student groups for Week 10 Participation 2 (2026-10-28).

  • Terms were converted into verbs in their imperative forms.

  • The raw list of student terms was deduplicated and grouped by intent.

  • Count = number of distinct groups that used the term or a close synonym (some noted in parentheses).

1. Data Preparation¶

*Cleaning, reshaping, and transforming raw data before analysis or visualization.

TermCountDefinitionSynonyms
Normalize3Apply a consistent scale or form across variables to enable valid comparisonStandardize
Transform3Convert data to a new representation or scale to reveal patterns or enable analysis
Sort3Order observations by the value of a variable
Clean1Remove errors, blanks, and inconsistencies to make data ready for analysis
Condense1Reduce the size or complexity of a dataset for efficiency
Combine1Merge two or more datasets or views into one
Reshape1Restructure data between long (tidy) and wide formatsTidy
Deduplicate1Eliminate repeated records from a datasetRemove Duplicates
Transpose1Swap rows and columns in a tabular structure
Map1Assign raw values to categories, bins, or identifiers
